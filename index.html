About Us

Pioneering Biomedical
Instrumentation Since 1996

K.S. Biomed Services is in the business of biomedical instrumentation. We offer services, technologies & products in various areas of medicine covering about 10 technologies and over 200 products.

We assist our clients through our total instruments management cycle — from selection of appropriate technology platforms, product specification & engineering cost estimates, to project engineering, installation, commissioning & maintenance.

Our strengths are our people & our concepts. We are a young multidisciplinary team who believe in building long-term relationships with our clients and work "with" rather than "for" them.
